Research objective

We are pursuing a separate CBD research program in relation to PTSD. This is a research objective, not a claim that CBD has been demonstrated to prevent, treat or cure PTSD.

The CBD program is separate from our kratom program and from our wholesale and private-label business.

What is not yet established

  • That CBD is an established treatment for PTSD.
  • That any CBDYNE product is FDA-approved or available for the treatment of PTSD.

Evidence standard

We do not represent any CBDYNE investigational product as FDA-approved, clinically proven, or available for the treatment of PTSD, opioid use disorder or any other disease.

Medical and regulatory notice: Nothing on this page is medical advice, a recommendation to use kratom or CBD, or an invitation to participate in a clinical trial. Do not stop or change prescribed treatment based on this information. For medical decisions, consult a qualified healthcare professional.
