Working with CBDYNE

What do you wholesale?

We offer CBD wholesale to qualified business customers. Kratom is part of our research programs only.

What other services do you offer?

We also offer botanical ingredients, laboratory and testing services, and consulting and regulatory services. See all capabilities

Is there a minimum order?

Yes. CBD is produced at the client’s request, and minimum orders apply. Minimums, formats and lead times are confirmed case by case.

Do you offer testing or consulting?

Yes, for our business partners. Testing support covers analytical testing and batch documentation. Consulting is for existing partners and covers seed-to-sale tracking, compliance and labeling. It is business guidance, not legal advice.

Laboratory & testing · Consulting & regulatory

Do you sell products to consumers?

No. CBDYNE focuses on business-to-business manufacturing, wholesale partnerships and research development. We are not a direct-to-consumer product shop.

Where do your partners operate?

Our wholesale and private-label activities serve business partners in California, Nevada and Arizona.

Where does your hemp come from?

We have hemp cultivation and sourcing activity in Colombia, with material brought into the United States for further processing or supply-chain use. We do not publish specific farm locations on this site.

Research

Is any CBDYNE product FDA-approved?

No. We do not represent any CBDYNE investigational product as FDA-approved, clinically proven, or available for the treatment of PTSD, opioid use disorder or any other disease.

What is an IND?

An Investigational New Drug (IND) application is a regulatory submission to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration seeking to conduct clinical investigations of an investigational drug. It is not a marketing approval and does not establish that the product is safe or effective.

When will human clinical trials begin?

We are targeting the start of human clinical trials for our kratom program in Q2 2027. We have not yet submitted an IND, received an IND effective date, or begun human trials.
