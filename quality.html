Documentation checklist

Depending on the product and transaction.

Documentation may include:

Documentation varies by material, batch and destination market. Please request current records for the specific material and batch you are considering.
  • Batch-specific certificates of analysis from an appropriately qualified independent laboratory.
  • Identity, composition and potency specifications.
  • Microbial, pesticide, heavy-metal and residual-solvent testing where relevant.
  • Country-of-origin, chain-of-custody and import/export documentation.
  • Manufacturing, packaging, storage and transport specifications.
  • Applicable permits, registrations and regulatory assessments.

Our approach

We treat traceability, batch identity and analytical documentation as essential parts of responsible ingredient supply.
