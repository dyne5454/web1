Quality and documentation

What partners should ask to see.

Before entering a supply relationship, partners should request and review documentation appropriate to the material and destination market. Depending on the product and transaction, this may include:

See the full documentation checklist

  • Batch-specific certificates of analysis from an appropriately qualified independent laboratory.
  • Identity, composition and potency specifications.
  • Country-of-origin, chain-of-custody and import/export documentation.
Documentation varies by material, batch and destination market. Please request current records for the specific material and batch you are considering.
