Scope

What testing can cover.

Testing is scoped to the material and to the partner’s needs. It can cover:

  • Potency and composition
  • Pesticides
  • Heavy metals
  • Microbial contaminants
  • Residual solvents

Clear records for every batch

Results are documented by batch, so partners keep a clear record of what they received.
